Privacy Policy for RemindMe

Last Updated: October 28, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of the RemindMe mobile application (the “App”).

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in privacy by design. The RemindMe app is built to be a private and local tool for you. We do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any personally identifiable information (PII) from you. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your location data.
  • Your device’s unique identifiers (other than for the purpose of processing payments through Google Play).
  • Any personal information from your Google account.

2. Information You Provide

  • Video Content: The video files you import into the App are stored locally on your own device. These files are accessed by the App solely for the purpose of playback as part of the reminder function. We do not have access to, nor do we upload, these files to any server. The responsibility for and ownership of this content remains entirely with you.
  • Reminder Plans: Information about your reminder schedules is also stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

3. In-App Purchases (Donations)

The App offers an option to make a voluntary donation to support development. These transactions are processed securely through the Google Play Billing service. We do not collect or store any of your financial information, such as credit card numbers. The information we receive from Google is limited to anonymized transaction data, which confirms that a purchase was made, but contains no personal information about you. For more details, please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

The RemindMe app may request the following permissions:

  • Read External Storage: To allow you to select and import video files into the app. The app only accesses the files you explicitly select.
  • Post Notifications: To send you scheduled reminders, which is the core function of the app.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google’s official services for core functionalities:

  • Google Play Billing: For processing in-app donations.
  • WorkManager: For scheduling notifications reliably.

These services are governed by Google’s own privacy policies.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the “Last Updated” date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at ya.app.help@gmail.com.
