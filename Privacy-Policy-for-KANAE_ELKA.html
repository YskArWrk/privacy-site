Privacy Policy for KANAE ELKA

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

KANAE ELKA ("the App") is an Android fantasy role-playing game developed by YA ("the Developer"), an independent app developer based in Japan.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, shared, and protected in connection with the App.

KANAE ELKA combines strategic defense battles, character development, equipment management, and storytelling.

The App is designed to allow users to enjoy the main game without creating an account or providing personal information.

The Developer respects user privacy and aims to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

KANAE ELKA does not require users to register an account or provide personally identifiable information to play the game.

The Developer does not directly request the following information during normal gameplay:

  • Your full name or physical address.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your email address for account registration.
  • Your contacts or address book.
  • Your precise GPS location.
  • Your personal photos or videos.
  • Your personal documents.
  • Your Google Account credentials.
  • Your payment card or banking information.

However, certain information may be collected automatically by third-party advertising services when those services are integrated into the App.

These practices are described in Section 4.

If you voluntarily contact the Developer by email, your email address and the information included in your message may be collected for communication purposes.

2. Game Data Stored on Your Device

KANAE ELKA is designed to store game progress and related information locally on your device.

This information may include:

  • Game Progress: Completed stages, Wave progression, achievements, and story progression.
  • Character Information: Character levels, attributes, abilities, classes, and development records.
  • Equipment and Inventory: Weapons, equipment upgrades, acquired items, and inventory information.
  • Game Resources: In-game currency, experience points, and other resources obtained through gameplay.
  • Gameplay Choices: Character selections, party configurations, and decisions affecting game progression.
  • Reward Records: Records of rewards received through gameplay or optional advertisements.
  • Game Settings: Language preferences, sound settings, and other gameplay configurations.

These records are used to save your progress, restore your game state, and maintain consistent gameplay.

Game data is intended to remain on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to third-party advertising services.

The Developer does not use locally stored game progress, character information, or story choices to create advertising profiles.

Certain device backup features may separately store application data depending on your Android settings.

3. Optional Rewarded Advertisements

KANAE ELKA is designed as a free-to-play game supported by optional rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements allow users to voluntarily watch advertisements in exchange for additional in-game benefits.

Depending on the game version, these benefits may include:

  • Additional experience points.
  • Additional in-game currency.
  • Additional equipment or item rewards.
  • Optional character development support.
  • Other optional gameplay rewards.

Watching rewarded advertisements is entirely voluntary.

Users can progress through the main story and reach the game's endings without watching advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements are intended to provide optional benefits rather than mandatory gameplay advantages.

The App may store reward confirmation records locally to prevent duplicate rewards and maintain accurate game progress.

These records may include reward types, reward amounts, confirmation status, and related timestamps.

Advertisement availability may depend on your internet connection, location, device settings, and advertisement inventory.

4. Advertising Services and Automatically Collected Information

KANAE ELKA plans to use Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display optional rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob is planned for integration into the production version of the App.

Once this service is integrated, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain information required for advertising services.

Depending on the device, advertising configuration, applicable laws, and user consent, this information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID.
  • IP address.
  • Device model and operating system information.
  • App interaction and usage information.
  • Advertisement impressions, clicks, and interactions.
  • Advertisement performance and diagnostic information.
  • General location information inferred from an IP address.
  • Technical information necessary to provide and secure advertising services.

The information actually collected depends on the advertising SDK, its configuration, and applicable privacy settings.

Advertising providers may use this information for purposes including:

  • Displaying advertisements.
  • Measuring advertisement performance.
  • Preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Maintaining service security.
  • Improving advertising services.
  • Providing personalized or non-personalized advertisements, where permitted.

Information processed by Google and its advertising partners is subject to their respective privacy policies.

For additional information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information for Advertising

6. Permissions

KANAE ELKA is designed to operate without requesting access to:

  • Your contacts.
  • Your precise location.
  • Your camera.
  • Your microphone.
  • Your personal photos and videos.
  • Your personal documents.

Internet access may be required for optional advertisement delivery, advertisement verification, and related third-party services.

The main gameplay is designed to remain available without an internet connection.

However, optional advertisement features may be unavailable while offline.

Actual application permissions may vary depending on the App version and integrated services.

  • Google AdMob: For optional rewarded advertisements and related advertising functionality.

Information handled by the App may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining gameplay functionality.
  • Saving and restoring game progress.
  • Managing in-game rewards and resources.
  • Preventing duplicate reward claims.
  • Delivering optional advertisements.
