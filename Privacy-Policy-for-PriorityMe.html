Privacy Policy for PriorityMe

Last Updated: October 28, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how PriorityMe (“the App”) handles your information. PriorityMe is designed to help you identify and execute your most important tasks without distraction. Because task management is a personal activity, the App prioritizes privacy from the ground up.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PriorityMe operates entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or access any personally identifiable information (PII). This includes:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or account information.
  • Your task names, priority settings, or any content you create in the App.
  • Your location data.
  • Your device identifiers (except minimal information required by Google Play Billing).
  • Any data from your Google Account.

2. Information You Provide in the App

Any information you enter into PriorityMe is stored locally on your device only. This includes:

  • Tasks & Priorities: All tasks, priority levels, and progress statuses are stored locally and remain under your control.
  • Focus Settings: Any custom configurations for how the App helps you choose the most important task remain on your device.

We cannot view or access this data. If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data is removed.

3. In-App Purchases (Optional Support)

PriorityMe offers optional in-app purchases for users who wish to support ongoing development. These purchases:

  • Are entirely voluntary.
  • Do not grant ownership, equity, or financial returns.
  • Are processed securely through Google Play Billing.

We do not collect or store your financial information. We only receive anonymized confirmation that a purchase occurred. For details, please review Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

PriorityMe does not request or use any special permissions. The App functions entirely without access to your files, contacts, location, or notification permissions.

5. Third-Party Services

PriorityMe uses the following official Google services:

  • Google Play Billing: For optional support purchases.

These services are governed by Google’s own privacy policies. PriorityMe does not transmit any task or priority data to third-party services.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When updates are made, we will revise the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ya.app.help@gmail.com.
